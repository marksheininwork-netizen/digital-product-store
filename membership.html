How access will work

Customers sign in, complete Stripe Checkout, and receive access only after a server-verified successful payment. Membership access will be checked by Supabase on each protected tool request; cancellations and expired subscriptions will remove membership-only access. One-time purchases will be recorded separately. Discounts will be enforced at checkout, not merely displayed in the browser.

Clean files

New downloadable products will be prepared as readable PDFs, editable DOCX files for Google Docs, or XLSX workbooks for Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets. Existing text, CSV, and ZIP products will be replaced only after the new files are created and tested.
