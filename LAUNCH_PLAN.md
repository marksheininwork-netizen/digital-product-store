# Northstar Studio — Launch plan

## Mission
One storefront for original downloadable digital products, automation kits, developer tools and subscription software. Excludes custom website-design services.

## MVP catalog (create real files before activating checkout)
1. Freelancer Invoice & Budget Kit — spreadsheet templates, instructions, sample data; one-time purchase.
2. Student Focus Planner — printable PDF and editable planner; one-time purchase.
3. Small Business Workflow Starter — original n8n workflow templates, setup instructions and sample data; one-time purchase.
4. Northstar Tools Membership — future hosted utilities; preview only until authenticated access, billing and actual features work.

## Release gates
- Real product artifacts with licenses, previews and download instructions.
- Server-side validated Stripe test Checkout; never trust prices from client.
- Verified signed webhook and idempotent order fulfillment.
- Private storage and expiring download links; durable order and entitlement records.
- Secure customer authentication, billing portal and cancellation handling.
- Automated tests for checkout, refund, subscription cancellation, unauthorized download and webhook replay.
- Accessible mobile QA, legal/refund/privacy pages, deployment and monitored logs.
- Production payments only after explicit account-owner approval and legal/account eligibility review.

## Portfolio expansion
Separate brands: business automation; education and games; consumer productivity; e-commerce; original digital media. Share reusable code, not customer data without permission.

## Success criteria
First verified end-to-end test purchase and download, then first legitimate customer purchase. Avoid claiming launch or revenue before verified.
