Three practical field guides

FREE GUIDE 1 · Four chapters

The Client Dashboard Handbook

Chapter 1

Define the decision before choosing metrics. A dashboard should tell a client what changed and what to do next. For lead generation, distinguish total inquiries from qualified opportunities.

Chapter 2

Define each metric with a numerator, denominator, data source and date range. For example, lead-to-sale conversion equals completed sales divided by qualified leads, not total website views.

Chapter 3

Design a report with a headline, trend, target, explanation and next action. Never use a chart without explaining the unit or time period.

Chapter 4

Run a monthly review: verify data, discuss two meaningful changes, identify one risk and assign one action with an owner and deadline.

FREE GUIDE 2 · Four chapters

The Channel Expansion Field Guide

Chapter 1

Score each potential channel for audience fit, production effort and ability to measure qualified interest. Begin with one channel rather than spreading resources thin.

Chapter 2

Create three content pillars: customer questions, practical demonstrations and real examples. Batch scripting, review and publishing without sacrificing accuracy.

Chapter 3

Run a 30-day test: baseline in week one, test one format in week two, improve the best example in week three, review useful outcomes in week four.

Chapter 4

Expand only after the first channel has a repeatable workflow. Adapt the same insight for different formats instead of copying identical posts everywhere.

FREE GUIDE 3 · Four chapters

The Digital Product Launch Manual

Chapter 1

Interview customers about a recent frustrating task. Choose a narrow deliverable such as a pricing worksheet or client intake checklist.

Chapter 2

Include an editable file, completed example, quick-start instructions, supported formats and clear limitations. Test realistic inputs and edge cases.

Chapter 3

Verify seller authorization, product claims, refund policy, privacy and payment fulfillment. Test checkout in sandbox before any real transaction.

Chapter 4

Track successful downloads, first-use completion, support requests and refunds. Fix the most frequent obstacle before adding more features.
