## Launch checklist
- [ ] Identify the customer and their problem
- [ ] Write a one-sentence value proposition
- [ ] Define deliverables and refund policy
- [ ] Create a simple portfolio example
- [ ] Set up a business contact method
- [ ] Confirm payment eligibility and tax obligations
- [ ] Publish a landing page
- [ ] Test inquiry and checkout workflows
- [ ] Ask first customers for feedback

## 30-day content plan
Week 1: Explain the problem, share a checklist, tell your story.
Week 2: Demonstrate your process, answer FAQs, show a before/after.
Week 3: Share a customer lesson, clarify pricing, publish a tutorial.
Week 4: Share results, address objections, invite a discovery call.

## Proposal outline
1. Customer objective
2. Scope and deliverables
3. Timeline and milestones
4. Price and payment schedule
5. Revision limits
6. Approval and next steps
