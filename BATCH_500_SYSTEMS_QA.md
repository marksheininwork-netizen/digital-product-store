# Batch 2 — 500 interactive business systems

## Delivered
The standalone `systems-500.html` page contains 500 configurations generated from:
- 10 audiences
- 10 business goals
- 5 functional product families

Families:
1. Client Performance Dashboard — 100 configurations, numeric KPI calculations
2. Channel Expansion System — 100 configurations, channel publishing targets and milestones
3. Client Pipeline & Delivery CRM — 100 configurations, prospect stages and ownership
4. Campaign Budget & ROI Planner — 100 configurations, cost per sale, revenue/cost and budget remaining
5. 90-Day Operations & Growth Plan — 100 configurations, 13-week action tracker

All products offer editable inputs, browser-local save, JSON export and print-to-PDF through the browser. This is **one reusable application with 500 preset configurations**, not 500 separately coded applications. It does not sync across devices, connect to client accounts, send messages, or integrate with real advertising APIs.

## Test checklist before publication
- [ ] Verify all 500 variants open and render in desktop browser
- [ ] Verify all 500 variants open and render on mobile
- [ ] Test every product family with sample data
- [ ] Verify calculations for zero denominators and negative budgets
- [ ] Verify local save, reload, reset, export, print
- [ ] Check keyboard access, screen reader labels, contrast and responsive layouts
- [ ] Check data retention notice and privacy policy
- [ ] Confirm no real client-sensitive information is included
- [ ] Editorially review each audience/goal preset and remove weak combinations
- [ ] Connect to store navigation only after checks pass

## Known limitations
- Dashboard metrics are manually entered; they are not live analytics.
- CRM is local-only and single-user.
- Channel expansion is a planning system, not automatic publishing.
- ROI is an illustrative calculation and excludes unentered costs.
- No payment/entitlement system is attached.
- No live deployment or end-to-end browser testing was performed as part of this batch.
